04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you begin.

What happens after my free trial?＋

After 3 days, your approved payment method is charged $10 USD monthly for three paid months. Starting with paid month four, the complete series renews at $30/month total ($10 per extension). Cancel before the next charge to avoid renewal.

Do I need three subscriptions?＋

No. This introductory offer is one subscription and one license unlocking all three studios. Keeping the complete series after the offer costs $30/month. Individual studio purchasing is not available in this version.

How do I install the extensions?＋

After subscription approval, download each studio from your account. Extract the ZIP, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Select the extracted folder containing manifest.json. Open License & activation in each studio and paste your Aurora license. These are unpacked packages; Chrome Web Store listings are not provided.

Can I cancel during the trial?＋

Yes, use your Aurora account to cancel before the trial ends. Stripe keeps access through the current trial or paid period. PayPal cancellation ends access immediately. A cancellation confirmation appears in your account.

Will Aurora guarantee follower growth?＋

No. Aurora provides browser workflow tools, not followers or guaranteed results. Platform rules and restrictions still apply. The studios pause on detected verification or restriction screens, and changing platform layouts can require extension updates.

What data leaves my browser?＋

Task settings, targets, history, and action receipts stay in the extension's local storage. License validation sends your license key and studio identifier to the Aurora server. Aurora stores account and subscription information; Stripe and PayPal handle your payment details. No social password is requested.
