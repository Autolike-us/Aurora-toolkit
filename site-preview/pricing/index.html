Pricing questions

How does the free trial work?

Create an account with your email — no card needed. You get 3 full days with all three extensions. When it ends, subscribe to keep going; nothing is charged automatically.

How much does it cost?

Launch offer: all 3 extensions for $10/month for the first 3 months after launch, and that price stays locked for as long as you stay subscribed. After the offer, each extension is $10/month, or $20/month for all three.

What happens to my launch price later?

If you subscribe during the launch window you keep the $10 all-3 price for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and come back after the offer, the regular price applies.

Which payment methods can I use?

Credit and debit cards through Stripe, or PayPal. You can cancel any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for.
