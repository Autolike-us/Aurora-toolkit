04 / Good questions

A little clarity
before you begin.

How does the free trial work?

Create an account with your email — no card needed. You get 3 full days with all three studios. When it ends, subscribe to keep going; nothing is charged automatically.

How much does it cost?

Launch offer: all 3 studios for $10/month for everyone who subscribes in the first 3 months after launch, and that price stays locked for as long as you stay subscribed. After the offer, each studio is $10/month, or $20/month for all three.

What happens to my launch price later?

If you subscribe during the launch window you keep the $10 all-3 price for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and come back after the offer, the regular price applies.

Which payment methods can I use?

Credit and debit cards through Stripe, or PayPal. You can cancel any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Will Aurora guarantee growth, or keep my accounts safe?

No tool can promise either. Aurora paces actions like a person, previews before it sends anything and stops on warnings or captchas — but Facebook, Instagram and TikTok restrict automated activity, so use sensible limits and read our platform disclaimer.

Does Aurora need my social passwords?

No. It runs inside your own browser, in the tab where you are already signed in. It never asks for or stores your social-network passwords, and your tasks and history stay in your browser.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave) on desktop. Install from the Chrome Web Store where available, or use the private download in about a minute.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

One license works on up to 3 browsers, so you can use it on your laptop and desktop.
