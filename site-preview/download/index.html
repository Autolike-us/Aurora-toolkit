Private download: step by step

Download & unzip

In My account, press “Download .zip” and unzip the file into a folder you will keep.

Load it in Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

Add your license

Click the Aurora icon, open the studio, and paste your license key into the License box. Your trial or plan unlocks immediately.

Updating? Download the new zip, replace the folder’s files, and press the reload icon on chrome://extensions.
