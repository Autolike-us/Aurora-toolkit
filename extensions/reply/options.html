    Keyboard shortcuts

    On Facebook, Instagram and TikTok pages. They only open and fill things — nothing is ever sent for you.

    Alt + ROpen or close the ✦ Reply panel
    Alt + 1 / 2 / 3Insert draft 1, 2 or 3 into the comment's reply box
    Alt + GWrite new drafts for the same comment
    Alt + NNext comment in the unanswered-comments queue
    EscClose the panel (when you are inside it)

    Good to know

    Why does it not send the reply for me?

    On purpose. You read and send every reply yourself — it keeps you in control and keeps your account safe.

    Nothing happens when I click “Use this reply”

    If the site's reply box can't be found, the reply is copied instead — click Reply on that comment and paste it. If it keeps happening, press “Report a problem” in the panel so we can fix it.

    My account is not in English

    Comments are found in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, Turkish and more. Drafts are written in the language of the comment, or the language you set in My voice.

    What counts as a draft?

    Each reply it writes counts as one. You choose 1, 2 or 3 replies per comment — pick 1 to use fewer. “Try it” and “Write new ones” count too. Quick replies are free.

    Your privacy

    • You stay in control. Aurora Reply only reads a comment when you ask for a draft, and it never presses Send.
    • What is sent: the comment you chose, the post's caption, and your voice settings go to Aurora's server, which asks an AI service (Anthropic) to write the drafts. Nothing else on the page is read.
    • What is kept: your history stays in this browser. If Voice backup is on (it is by default), your voice profiles and quick replies are also saved under your key so you can restore them. Our server otherwise keeps only a count of drafts for your key — not the comments or the replies.
    • Please: don't use it on private messages. It is built for public comments. Replies are suggestions — check them before you send, and follow each platform's rules.
