Your privacy

  • You stay in control. Aurora Reply only reads a comment when you ask for a draft, and it never presses Send.
  • What is sent: the comment you chose, the post's caption, and your voice settings above go to Aurora's server, which asks an AI service (Anthropic) to write the drafts. Nothing else on the page is read.
  • What is kept: your voice settings stay in this browser. Our server keeps only a count of drafts for your key — not the comments or the replies.
  • Please: don't use it on private messages. It is built for public comments. Replies are suggestions — check them before you send, and follow each platform's rules.
